# Sales-Revenue-Analysis-Dashboard

1.Project Discription 

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes sales and revenue using date-wise, product-wise, category-wise, and region-wise data. It uses colorful charts and visualizations to identify sales trends, top-performing products, and high-performing regions.

2.Objectives

Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
Track date-wise and monthly revenue trends.
Identify top-performing products.
Compare sales across different categories.
Analyze regional sales performance.
Provide easy-to-understand visual insights.
Support better business decision-making.

3.Dataset

The dataset contains 200 sales records with information such as:
Order ID
Date
Product
Category
Region
Quantity
Unit Price
Discount
Discount Amount
Sales
Revenue
Salesperson
Payment Method

4.Dashboard Features

📌 Total Sales
📌 Total Revenue
📌 Total Orders
📌 Total Quantity
📈 Monthly Revenue Trend
📊 Sales by Product
🥧 Sales by Category
📊 Sales by Region
📅 Date-wise analysis
🔎 Interactive filtering/analysis

5. Charts Used
📊 Bar Chart
Sales by Product
Used to compare the performance of different products.
🥧 Pie Chart
Sales by Category
Used to understand the contribution of each category to total sales.
📊 Column/Bar Chart
Sales by Region
Used to compare sales performance across different regions.
📈 Line Chart
Monthly Revenue Trend
Used to identify changes and trends in revenue over time.
